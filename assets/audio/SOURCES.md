# S05 audio

All sounds are original procedural PCM generated by `scripts/presentation/effects/combat_audio_bank.gd`: low-frequency decaying noise for heavy guns/impacts, a short higher-frequency report for light guns, and a periodic harmonic engine loop. No external recordings or third-party assets. The effects consumer owns at most eight one-shot sources and four loop sources. Godot's 3D distance attenuation applies. These are deliberately simple effects, not recordings of specific historical weapons.
