# Type 94 46 cm triple turret housing

The reusable scene is `turret_housing.tscn`. Its root is the train-axis origin and its geometry is generated by `scripts/naval/weapons/turret_housing_module.gd` from the shared main-turret profile. Each station follows `Foundation_<station_id>/WeaponInstance_<station_id>/TrainPivot_<station_id>/HousingAndControls`; all three main turrets instance this same housing scene, while each fixed roller-path foundation remains attached to its ship station.

The scene contains the rotating lower ring, armored housing, turret-top rails, rangefinder fittings, hatches, and ladder details. Elevating barrels and their trunnion parts remain under the weapon's independent elevation axis.

The source-backed dimensional inputs are recorded in `data/weapon_profiles.json` and `docs/configuration-1944.md` from USNTMJ Report O-45(N), printed page 17. The outside armor shell, port dimensions, roof attachments, rail height, and exposed barrel length remain image-proportioned estimates. Appearance detail cues are recorded in `docs/references/appearance-photo-galleries.md`.

Geometry is generated from project code; no external mesh or texture assets are included. No third-party asset license applies to this procedural mesh.
