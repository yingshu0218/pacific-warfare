# Historical basis — USS Iowa BB-61, 28 December 1944

Research started 2026-11-06; S02 update 2026-11-10. Current source inspection and station tables are in [the dated S02 reference brief](../../docs/references/ships/iowa_bb61_1944_12_28/modeling-brief.md). Research-only Navy photographs and public manufacturer instruction pages are now retained there, outside runtime assets. Earlier source links below retain their original date and variant restrictions.

## Why this ship and date

The existing repository describes an American/Japanese Pacific naval battle but contains no American ship scenario or Iowa data. The first prototype is therefore **USS Iowa (BB-61)** at the dated condition shown in official U.S. Navy photograph **80-G-421124**, an aerial photograph made in the floating dry dock at Manus on 28 December 1944. This provides a ship-specific, date-specific top-side view for checking the arrangement. The January 1944 photograph 80-G-235080 confirms Iowa's Measure 32 Design 1B appearance earlier that year. The Bureau of Ships camouflage drawing 80-G-105514 is for Design 7A, a different Iowa-class scheme that was not used; it is not used here as a hull profile or configuration reference.

This supports one elevation axis per barrel in the main turret scene; it is not used as evidence for the ship's dated exterior fit.
- [Bureau of Ordnance, OP-769, 16-Inch Three-Gun Turrets, Part 1](https://eugeneleeslover.com/ENGINEERING/OP769/OP-769_Part1.pdf) — a 1968 reactivation manual for USS New Jersey (BB-62). - [Bureau of Ordnance, OP-1112, Gun Mount and Turret Catalog](https://www.eugeneleeslover.com/ENGINEERING/OP-1112.php) — second revision dated 7 March 1945; includes the Mark 28 Mod 2 mount data sheet. - [Official Battleship Iowa Crew Handbook](https://mathscinotes.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/IowaCrewHandbook.pdf) — identifies the Iowa-class enclosed twin 5-inch mount as Mark 28 Mod 2 and describes the 5-inch/38 gun. Used as a class-level identification, not as an installation plan.
- [NHHC DANFS: Iowa III (BB-61)](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/histories/ship-histories/danfs/i/iowa-iii.html) — service timeline and broad ship dimensions.
- [National Archives ship plans guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/cartographic/ship-plans) — describes the Navy ship-plan holdings and typical engineering drawing types. No unpublished NARA plan is claimed as consulted for this model.

## Current geometry and station evidence (S02)

The current model is the 28 December 1944 BB-61 condition. S02 actually opened USN/NARA 80-G-421124, the previous-day bow views 80-G-304057/58, 80-G-358652, and January views 80-G-235080/84, together with all three pages of Tamiya 31616's 1944 instructions. Their files, viewing directions, limitations and per-station evidence are in the linked S02 brief and sources.json. The manufacturer plan is a source-matched visual layout, not a surveyed Navy installation drawing.

The earlier -91/-59/+91 metre main stations and broadly spread secondary stations were wrong. Main centers now follow the 1944 plan near -58.798/-33.096/+66.896 metres. All three triple 16-inch turrets and all ten twin Mk28 5-inch mounts remain. The latter form three elevated inner and two lower outer stations per side, on a continuous upper battery deck and weather deck respectively. S02 changed the hull's fine deck-edge envelope and raked stem, the round armored conning tower/open navigation gallery, the narrow rounded forward director-support tower, two independent funnels and their full openings, and the after director/mast relationship. These replace the earlier broad stepped bridge study and eleven-window approximation. No 1945 enclosed navigation bridge is claimed.

All foundations originate at declared host faces; the tests ray the host mesh while excluding each foundation itself. Main gunhouse side, back and front armor between ports is separately checked. The 16-inch gunhouse envelope and independent gun elevation retain the shared-class OP-769/Navy ordnance basis listed above; later New Jersey details still do not prove Iowa's dated exterior. Unneeded cylinder height rings and small static parts are reduced/batched without joining different stations or motion axes.

The current **game** fit has 19 quad Bofors and 26 single Oerlikons. The previous working 19/52 historical assumption is retained as an uncertain baseline, not an exact-date claim. All game AA sits on fixed hull/battery-deck hosts. Turret-roof AA is omitted from this game fit; 5-inch dual-purpose secondary mounts are retained in full. This is a separate dedicated-AA decision, not evidence for a new historical configuration.

Two catapults and one stern aircraft-recovery crane remain, with image-proportioned centers and mechanisms. Floatplanes are deferred. Procedural Measure32 Design1B appearance is retained; exact patch geometry and colors remain uncertain, and the two sides still share an authored approximation. Its original SVG is embedded as a shared runtime image resource so root and standalone projects do not overwrite each other's import metadata.

## Dimensions and uncertainty

LOA270.4m, maximum beam33m and draft11.3m are rounded wartime dimensions. Hull transverse sections, intermediate sheer, absolute bridge/platform elevations, bulb/stern/appendage sections and smaller equipment remain image-proportioned rather than shipyard offsets. The main/secondary station ordering, barrel counts, centerline/side relationships and deck tiers are explicit and source checked; absolute numeric elevations are not claimed as a surveyed installation plan.

Runtime data uses `data/units/us_iowa_bb61_1944_12_28.json` in the root project. Its pure model scene contains no camera, UI or autonomous simulation. All 58 sockets and actual muzzle markers are compared field by field. Cycle, round allocation, rates and descriptive ammunition defaults remain provisional for S05 research; S02 implements no firing, ballistic or damage mechanism. Browser acceptance is S08 work.
