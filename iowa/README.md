# USS Iowa BB-61 model

Standalone Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility project for an independently instantiable, procedural USS Iowa model. It does not replace the repository's main scene or edit the Yamato study.

## Launch

From the repository root:

```sh
GODOT=/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot
"$GODOT" --path iowa
```

Run the ship component in another scene with `res://scenes/iowa_bb61.tscn`. For isolated inspection captures:

```sh
"$GODOT" --path iowa res://scenes/iowa_viewer.tscn -- --view top --capture /tmp/iowa-top.png --hide-ui
"$GODOT" --path iowa res://scenes/iowa_viewer.tscn -- --view side --capture /tmp/iowa-side.png --hide-ui
"$GODOT" --path iowa res://scenes/iowa_viewer.tscn -- --view bridge --capture /tmp/iowa-bridge.png --hide-ui
"$GODOT" --path iowa res://scenes/iowa_viewer.tscn -- --view turret --capture /tmp/iowa-turret.png --hide-ui
```

`--view` supports `overview`, `top`, `side`, `bridge`, `turret`, `bow`, `stern`, `aircraft`, `catapult`, and `crane`. The stern aircraft-handling views are also available on keys `7`–`9`. Captures are written outside the project by default.

First-version native-render captures are saved under [docs/screenshots](docs/screenshots/): overview, top, side, bridge, turret, and stern.

## Viewer controls

- Left drag: orbit. Right or middle drag: pan. Wheel: zoom.
- `1` overview, `2` top, `3` side, `4` bridge, `5` forward turret, `6` secondary mount, `7` stern aircraft-handling overview, `8` catapult, `9` crane, `R` reset.
- `Tab` selects a mount. `Q/E` selects a gun within it. `A/D` commands turret train; `W/S` elevates only the selected gun. `Space` pauses the fixed-step demonstration. `H` hides the HUD.
- Every weapon exposes a `TrainPivot` and one or more `ElevationPivot` nodes, limits, drive-rate fields, `aim_at_train_angle()`, `aim_at_gun_elevation()`, and `resume_demo()`.

## Selected configuration and limits

The first representation is USS Iowa (BB-61) on **28 December 1944**, the date of the NHHC aerial photograph 80-G-421124. The exterior has a lofted hull, fore-and-aft deck sheer, stepped forward tower, bridge wings, after director tower, two separately modeled funnels with independent uptake modules, two rotating stern catapult trusses, one centerline aircraft-recovery crane, four shafts, screws, rudders, deck fittings, and separate weapon assemblies.

| Mount family | Count | Assembly state |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 16-inch/50 three-gun turret | 3 | One reusable scene resource; separate barbette foundation; one train axis and three independent gun-elevation pivots. |
| 5-inch/38 twin turret | 10 | One reusable scene; each station has its own foundation, train axis, and elevation axis. |
| 40 mm quad Bofors | 19 | One reusable scene; each station has its own foundation, train axis, and elevation axis. |
| 20 mm single Oerlikon | 26 | One reusable scene; each station has its own foundation, train axis, and elevation axis. |

The table describes the current digital model (58 mounts total), not a dated inventory of the real ship. The standalone HUD derives its counts from the instantiated assembly.

The runtime ship hierarchy groups `Hull`, `Superstructure`, `MountFoundations`, and `Equipment`; the superstructure is split into bridge, platforms, mast, funnel, and after-tower modules. Every station marker records its host module, local position, interface name, source status, and confidence. Each weapon family has a reusable scene and a separate foundation with a named `WeaponSeat`; light-AA foundations are children of their station markers, so roof mounts continue to follow their host turret. The hierarchy is now inspectable, while most Hull, Superstructure, and Equipment geometry is still generated by `iowa_ship.gd` rather than reusable external scenes.

Dimensions, mount centers, many absolute heights, AA count, radar and aircraft details are not all confirmed for that exact date. `data/december_1944.json` marks the position and motion parameters as estimates. The light-AA counts and most individual centers are not a survey. Do not treat the model as a shipyard plan or a finished historical reconstruction. See [historical basis](docs/historical_basis.md), [module migration ledger](docs/module_migration.md), and [validation](docs/validation.md).

The current weapon-module pass and close views are recorded in [weapon module pass](docs/weapon_module_pass.md). Small-mount foundation dimensions remain visual estimates pending a dated installation drawing.

## Visual scope

The viewer uses neutral inspection lighting, an approximation of the Measure 32 camouflage, a flat low-detail ocean, and simplified part shapes to make the exterior readable. It does not reproduce an exact surveyed paint pattern, exact deck planking, rivets, welds, internal compartments, or production-level weapon mechanisms. No third-party 3D models, photographs, scanned plans, textures, or generated images are included in this project.
